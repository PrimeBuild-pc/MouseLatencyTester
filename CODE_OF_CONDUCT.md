# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about measurements, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep criticism technical and specific. "This adds 40 µs to the ISR" is
  useful; "this is bad code" is not.
- Assume contributors have less context than you, not less intelligence.
- Respect that people work on this in their spare time, in many languages and
  across time zones. A slow reply is not a snub.
- Back claims about timing or performance with numbers. This is a measurement
  project; it is the whole point.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument after a decision
  has been made and explained.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other project space.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **primebuild.official@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations. Enforcement decisions
are explained where doing so does not expose the reporter.
